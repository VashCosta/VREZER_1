# CareerForge AI – AI Career Intelligence Platform Vision & Guidelines

## Core Identity & Vision
CareerForge AI is NOT a basic resume scanner. It is a next-generation, production-ready, enterprise-level **AI Career Intelligence & Employability Ecosystem** designed for students, freshers, job-seekers, recruiters, colleges, and enterprise talent acquisitions.

## Visual & UX Standards
- **Aesthetic**: Premium SaaS product UI benchmarked against Notion, Linear, Stripe Dashboard, Clerk, Vercel, and LinkedIn.
- **Design System**: Glassmorphism, luminous gradient meshes, ultra-clean dark/light mode toggle, dynamic Recharts visualizations, micro-interactions with Framer Motion, professional typography, responsive sidebar navigation.

## Key System Pillars
1. **Multi-Role Ecosystem**: Student, Recruiter, Admin.
2. **AI Resume & ATS Intelligence**: Parse PDF/DOCX, generate ATS score, flag formatting flaws (icons, tables, columns, headers, margins), missing keywords, grammar, section improvements.
3. **Career Acceleration**: Job Description Matcher, Target Company Skill Gap Analyzer (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Netflix, Adobe, etc.), Interactive Career Roadmap Generator.
4. **Interview & Mock AI Studio**: Role/difficulty technical/behavioral/system design question generator + live interactive Mock Interview engine.
5. **Career Tools**: Compensation & Salary Predictor, Portfolio/GitHub/LinkedIn Health Auditor, Resume-boosting Project Recommender, Multi-template Live Resume Builder (PDF export), Cover Letter Generator, LinkedIn Profile Optimizer.
6. **Recruiter & Admin Suite**: Batch multi-resume upload, skill search & candidate ranking, shortlisted candidate reports, administrative analytics & system logs.
7. **AI Career Coach Chatbot**: Floating assistant offering instant career guidance and quick action prompts.
